Frequently Asked Questions

Both! We offer mobile detailing where we come to your home, office, or wherever your car is parked, so you just need to provide access to water and a power outlet nearby. We also have a shop location at 1325 Isabelle Circle if you prefer to drop it off.
Details can range anywhere from 3–5 hours depending on the service you choose, the current condition of the vehicle, and its size. I never rush. Your car gets the time it needs to be done right.
Just two things: access to a water spigot and a standard 120V outdoor power outlet within a reasonable distance of your vehicle. I bring everything else: equipment, products, buckets, everything.
On rainy days, interior-only services can still go ahead as scheduled. For any service that includes exterior work, I'll need either a covered space like a garage or carport to work under, or we'll reschedule for a dry day. Washing and rinsing a car in the rain doesn't work. The results just won't be what you're paying for.
My goal is for you to be genuinely happy with the result. If you're not satisfied with any part of the detail, let me know before I leave and I'll address it on the spot. If a specific concern is raised after the fact, I'll work with you to make it right. Refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis. I'm not here to take your money and disappear. I'm here to earn your trust.
Please remove any personal belongings from the car beforehand: things like bags, chargers, kids' stuff, loose change, documents, etc. It helps me get into every area without having to move your things around, and makes sure nothing gets misplaced. Beyond that, just make sure I have access to the vehicle and we're good to go.
I ask for at least 24–48 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. That gives me enough time to adjust my schedule and offer the slot to someone else. Life happens and I'm always willing to work with you, just reach out as early as you can.
I accept CashApp, Zelle, Venmo, Apple Pay, PayPal, and good old-fashioned cash. Payment is due at the time of service.
Fill out the booking form above and I'll reach out to confirm a time that works for you. You can also call or text me directly at (650) 219-3149. I usually respond within a few hours.
Meet Braedon

Real Care for Your Vehicle — My Story

I'm Braedon. Back during COVID, I was a full-on germophobe. My room had to be spotless, everything in its place, not a single thing out of order. That level of meticulousness stuck with me, and over time it naturally shifted into something I'm actually proud of: an obsession with clean cars.

The business itself started out of necessity. One day I checked my bank account and had $7.42 — not enough for a bucket of balls at the driving range. Instead of sitting around, I grabbed a bucket, some car soap, microfiber towels, and a wash mitt, and started knocking on doors around my neighborhood offering a quick $20 car wash. That's how this whole thing started.

I don't carry the same germophobe energy these days, but that same eye for detail and zero tolerance for "good enough" absolutely carries over into every detail I do. When I'm working on your car, I treat it like it's mine.

I'm heading to Santa Clara University this fall, but this business is real and it's not going anywhere. I show up on time, I work hard, and I don't leave until the job's done right. I'm building this one happy customer at a time and I'd love to earn yours.
