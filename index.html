Meet Braedon

Real Care for Your Vehicle — My Story

I'm Braedon. Back during COVID, I was a full-on germophobe. My room had to be spotless, everything in its place, not a single thing out of order. That level of meticulousness stuck with me, and over time it naturally shifted into something I'm actually proud of: an obsession with clean cars.

I don't carry the same germophobe energy these days, but that same eye for detail and zero tolerance for "good enough" absolutely carries over into every detail I do. When I'm working on your car, I treat it like it's mine.

I'm heading to Santa Clara University this fall, but this business is real and it's not going anywhere. I show up on time, I work hard, and I don't leave until the job's done right. I'm building this one happy customer at a time and I'd love to earn yours.
